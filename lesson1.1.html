Vocabulary: Tech & Social Media

The following words and phrases are essential for discussing technology and modern lifestyle. Study their meanings, pronunciation, and example sentences.

Vocabulary Meaning
Digital native
/ˈdɪdʒɪtl ˈneɪtɪv/
A person born during the age of digital technology who is familiar with computers and the internet from an early age.
"My niece is a true digital native—she can use a tablet before she could read."
To go viral
/tə ɡoʊ ˈvaɪrəl/
To spread rapidly and widely on the internet through social media and other online platforms.
"The funny cat video went viral and got millions of views in just 48 hours."
Work-life balance
/wɜːrk laɪf ˈbæləns/
The balance between professional responsibilities and personal life activities.
"Many companies now promote better work-life balance by offering flexible working hours."
Screen time
/skriːn taɪm/
The amount of time spent using a device with a screen such as a smartphone, computer, or television.
"Doctors recommend limiting screen time before bed to improve sleep quality."
To be on the go
/tə bi ɒn ðə ɡoʊ/
To be constantly busy and active, moving from one activity or place to another.
"Modern lifestyle keeps us constantly on the go with little time for relaxation."

Create elaborate sentences using new words.
  1. A "digital native" is someone born into the digital age who grew up with technology, while a "digital immigrant" is someone who adopted technology later in life.
    Digital natives are typically more comfortable with technology as it has always been part of their lives.
  2. Examples include: funny videos, inspirational stories, breaking news, controversial opinions, or creative content that captures public attention.
    Viral content usually evokes strong emotions (humor, surprise, anger) or provides value that people want to share.

Reading: The Internet Revolution

The Internet has already entered our ordinary life. Everybody knows that the Internet is a global computer network, which embraces hundred of millions of users all over the world and helps us to communicate with each other.

The history of Internet began in the United States in 1969. It was a military experiment, designed to help survive during a nuclear war, when everything around might be polluted by radiation and it would be dangerous to get out for any living being to get some information to anywhere. Information sent over the Internet takes the shortest and safest path available from one computer to another. Because of this, any two computers on the net will be able to stay in touch with each other as long as there is a single route between them. This technology was called packet switching. Invention of modems, special devices allowing your computer to send the information through the telephone line, has opened doors to the Internet for millions of people.

Most of the Internet host computers are in the United States of America. It is clear that the accurate number of users can be counted fairly approximately, nobody knows exactly how many people use the Internet today, because there are billions of users and their number is growing.

In many countries, the Internet could provide businessmen with a reliable, alternative to the expensive and unreliable telecommunications systems its own system of communications. Commercial users can communicate cheaply over the Internet with the rest of the world. When they send e-mail messages, they only have to pay for phone calls to their local service providers, not for international calls around the world, when you pay a good deal of money.

But saving money is only the first step and not the last one. There is a commercial use of this network and it is drastically increasing. Now you can work through the internet, chat with friends and play through the net.

However, there are some problems. The most important problem is security. When you send an e-mail, your message can travel through many different networks and computers. The data is constantly being directed towards its destination by special computers called routers. Because of this, it is possible to get into any of the computers along the route, intercept and even change the data being sent over the Internet. But there are many encoding programs available.

Another big and serious problem of the net is control. Yes, there is no effective control in the Internet, because a huge amount of information circulating through the net. It is like a tremendous library and market together. That is why you should be careful, while searching the net. Things like viruses or other digital malware at large in the net. Because of this you should learn some basic rules for using the Internet. Such us: Don’t click unknown links, don’t share your personal information online and don’t talk to people you don’t know.

Writing: Opinion Essay

Writing Task

Write a small essay(5 – 10 sentences) about the Internet, include 2 advantages of it and 2 downsides.

Tip: Try to use at least 3 vocabulary words from this lesson in your response.

Word count: 0
Writing Structure
Introduction: State your opinion clearly
Body: 2-3 supporting arguments with examples
Conclusion: Summarize your main points
Word count: Aim for 120-150 words

Project: Digital Diary

Project: Digital Habits Diary

Track your digital device usage for 3 days and analyze your habits to become more mindful of your screen time.

  1. Create a simple table to track your screen time across different devices
  2. Note what activities you do on each device (work, social media, entertainment, etc.)
  3. Identify any patterns or areas of excessive usage
  4. Write a short report (200-250 words) with your findings and observations
  5. Suggest one practical change you could make to improve your digital habits
Project Tips
• Use a notebook or digital document to track your usage
• Be honest about your screen time
• Look for patterns (e.g., most usage in evenings)
• Set realistic goals for improvement
