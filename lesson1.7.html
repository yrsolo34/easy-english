Phrasal Verb: TO CARRY

Phrasal VerbMeaningExample
carry oncontinuePlease carry on with your work.
carry outperform/executeThe scientists carried out an experiment.
carry offwin / handle successfullyShe carried off the first prize.
carry overpostpone / transferThe meeting was carried over to next week.
carry throughcomplete despite difficultiesHis determination carried him through.

Discussion Questions

1 Do you use AI in your daily life? How?
2 Can AI be dangerous? Why/why not?
3 Will AI replace human jobs? Which ones?
Speaking Tips
• Use Present Simple for facts: "AI helps people..."
• Use Modal verbs for opinions: "AI might be dangerous because..."
• Use Future forms for predictions: "AI will replace some jobs."
