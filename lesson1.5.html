Lesson 1.5

Privacy vs. Entertainment

In today's digital world, we often sacrifice privacy for entertainment through social media and streaming services. This lesson explores the challenging balance between protecting our personal data and enjoying viral content and online fun.

Reading: What is an Influencer?

The internet has become an essential part of everyone’s lives as well as the global economic climate. Recently, the worldwide web has been dominated by the creativity of social networking technologies and blogs. Today, a sizable portion of the global population uses social networking platforms to communicate with friends and family, as well as to share their whole lives on the internet.

The regular social media websites, such as Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram, not only allow people to build profiles. Many other websites need registration as well as the construction of a profile. As a result of all these, people are going to certainly post their private information on these sites, and for that reason, it often becomes accessible to any individual worldwide as it is on the internet. This may turn into a severe concern with regards to identity theft, and may additionally result in theft as well as stalking. People can utilize your private information and attempt to obtain personal, crucial numbers associated with bank accounts or credit cards, which may consequently result in theft in severe cases. In the case of stalking, though perhaps not too frequent, individuals having mental problems or an abnormal infatuation with another individual can make use of the private information as well as daily activities that a person shares on the internet to go to the extreme conditions of stalking them.

There are lots of adverse implications associated with sharing information on the internet, but people take pleasure in keeping in touch with families and friends regardless of physical distance, as well as keeping themselves updated in relation to essential functions in their lifestyles. Social networking sites are not a negative element, but they ought to be utilized in a way that a person’s privacy is secured. To guarantee better online privacy, social media websites ought to provide enhanced privacy alternatives, and individuals, as well as parents, need to educate themselves as well as other people on the way to maintain their data security whenever browsing the internet.
